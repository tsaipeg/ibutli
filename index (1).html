01

為啥物用台語學物理 Why physics in Taiwanese

A language stays alive when it can talk about everything — not only food and family, but momentum, magnetism and light. Most of us were taught science only in Mandarin or English, so Tâi-gí never grew the words. This project builds them, out loud, together.

聽有 Understand it first time

Ideas land faster in the language you dream in. We teach the concept in Tâi-gí, then anchor every term to English so nothing gets lost.

傳落去 Keep the language living

Like Te Reo Māori revitalisation, a heritage tongue survives by taking on new territory. Coining and sharing physics vocabulary is preservation in action.

做伙 Learn together

Open study groups and workshops mean you're never decoding equations alone. Beginners, heritage speakers and teachers all welcome.
